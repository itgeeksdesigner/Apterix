The Cabin Experience

Tint isn't just darker glass. It's a calmer cabin.

APTERIX:SLR+ is a multi-layer nano-ceramic film. Instead of darkening the glass with dye or metalizing, microscopic ceramic particles reject heat and UV while staying optically clean — so the driving experience is cooler and quieter without changing how the car looks from the outside.

  • Lower cabin temperatures at the seat, not just at the glass.
  • Reduced glare in early-morning and late-afternoon driving.
  • 99% UV blocked — skin and interior trim both stay protected.
  • Privacy without the mirror-finish "limo" look.
