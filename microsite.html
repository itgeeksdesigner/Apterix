The Story

Engineered from the molecule up. Refined on every install bay.

The Polycarbonate Edition is more than a product release — it's the result of three years of materials research, real-world installer feedback, and a refusal to ship anything we wouldn't put on our own cars.

Where most PPF brands license a film and re-skin the marketing, we built the chemistry. The polymer matrix, the adhesive layer, and the self-healing topcoat are all designed in-house, then validated across thousands of installs in every climate we could find.

  • Three years of in-house R&D, $2M+ invested since 2023.
  • Tested across desert, coastal, and alpine climates.
  • Validated by 200+ certified installers before release.
