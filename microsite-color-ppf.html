Transformation, Protected

Why wrap, when you can wrap and protect at the same time?

Standard vinyl wraps change a color, but they don't protect against rock chips, abrasion, or chemical etch. APTERIX:CLR is full paint protection film with the color built in — so every panel gets both transformation and impact protection without an extra layer.

  • Same self-healing topcoat as our flagship PPF.
  • Color depth verified against OEM gloss standards.
  • Removable for resale without paint damage.
  • Available for full wraps or accent panels.
