The Story

From installation bay to molecular lab.

A three-year journey to build the first polycarbonate-dominant paint protection film designed end-to-end by installers, for installers.

Chapter One

It started with a film we couldn't trust.

In 2022, Apterix was still a family-run installation studio. We installed every premium PPF brand on the market — and watched them fail in ways no marketing brochure would admit. Yellowing within three years. Adhesive blooms after the first hot summer. Orange-peel haze that no amount of polishing could hide.

We did what installers always do: we adapted. We layered topcoats, swapped adhesives, and built our own pre-cut patterns. But the underlying film was never ours. We were just re-skinning someone else's compromise.

By the end of that year, we'd had enough. We sold the install bay's busiest week of bookings — and used the money to commission a polymer chemist.

If we were going to put our name on it, the molecule had to be ours. Everything else is just packaging.

Founder, Apterix Installer for 14 years
Chapter Two

The chemistry — and the patent.

The breakthrough came from rethinking the dominant material in the film matrix. Most PPF is built on TPU — a urethane that's flexible and forgiving, but compromises on optical clarity and long-term yellowing resistance.

Our team developed a polycarbonate-dominant matrix: a multi-layer construction where polycarbonate carries the optical and impact load, while a refined adhesive base handles conformability. The result is a film that's harder, clearer, and dramatically more resistant to UV-driven discoloration.

In 2025, the US Patent & Trademark Office granted protection for the chemistry. The Polycarbonate Edition is the first commercial release built entirely on that patent.

The Process

Four years, four discipline.

Every Polycarbonate Edition kit is the result of work across four distinct phases — none of which were outsourced.

Materials Research

Polymer chemists develop the polycarbonate-dominant matrix in our in-house lab.

Adhesive Engineering

A custom acrylic adhesive layer is tuned to conform without compromising the polycarbonate core.

Installer Validation

200+ certified installers stress-test every batch on real vehicles, real climates, real timelines.
