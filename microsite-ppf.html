How It Protects

An invisible armor layer that flexes, heals, and stays optically clear.

Most paint protection films are PU-based and yellow within a few years. APTERIX:PRO uses a polycarbonate-dominant matrix — the same family of polymers used in fighter-jet canopies and bullet-resistant glass — tuned for automotive surfaces.

  • Absorbs rock and gravel impact instead of transferring it to paint.
  • Resists yellowing and clouding under UV exposure.
  • Self-healing topcoat removes minor swirls with heat.
  • Removable without damage to factory clear coat.
