Built for the Water

An automotive film won't last on a boat. APTERIX:AQUA is built for marine duty.

Marine surfaces don't get the same treatment as automotive paint. Hulls and topsides live under direct sun, salt fog, fuel spray, and constant water contact — conditions that destroy automotive-grade films within a season. APTERIX:AQUA is formulated specifically for that environment.

  • Hulls, topsides, and high-impact gelcoat areas.
  • Water-sports vessels — wake, ski, and tow boats.
  • Yacht trim, swim platforms, and edge-prone panels.
  • Saltwater fishing rigs and offshore craft.
